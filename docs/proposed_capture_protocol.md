# Proposed Capture Protocol for a 4D Dental Virtual Patient Dataset

## Minimal capture

1. **Facial video**: 30–60 seconds, stable illumination, neutral background.
2. **Expressions**: rest, social smile, maximum smile, lip purse, cheek puff, mouth opening/closing.
3. **Head motion**: slow left/right rotation and slight up/down movement.
4. **Speech**: predefined short phrases containing labial, dental and sibilant phonemes.
5. **Dental scans**: upper IOS, lower IOS and buccal bite scan.
6. **Design mesh**: original dentition plus proposed smile/restorative STL/PLY.

## Optional advanced capture

- CBCT when clinically indicated
- jaw-motion tracking
- calibrated facial scan
- color-calibrated dental photography

## Privacy

Facial video and 3D face geometry are identifiable biometric data. Obtain specific consent, ethics approval where applicable, controlled storage and de-identification/pseudonymization procedures.
